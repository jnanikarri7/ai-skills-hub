About the Role

What is an AI Data Engineer?

The bridge between data infrastructure and production ML.

AI Data Engineers build the pipelines, feature stores, and data infrastructure that power machine learning systems in production. They sit between traditional data engineering and ML Engineering.

Unlike a classic DE who focuses on reliable pipelines and warehouses, an AI DE must also understand model training workflows, feature engineering, vector databases, LLM integration patterns, and MLOps tooling.

This hub helps you close those skill gaps — whether you're coming from data engineering, analytics, or another technical role.

Who transitions to this role

Core responsibilities
⚙️Build scalable feature pipelines for ML training and serving
🔍Instrument data quality checks and observability
🚀Deploy, monitor, and maintain ML models in production
🤖Integrate LLMs, RAG pipelines, and vector search
📡Build real-time streaming for AI feature computation
